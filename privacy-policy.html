Rapid Realms: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Rapid Realms ("the game") is made by NassimDroid. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The game itself does not collect, store or share any personal information. It can show optional ads, described below.

What the game stores

Your progress is saved only on your own device: Shards, best depth, charm levels, vessels, badges, Carillon stage stars, settings and play statistics. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or any other personal information.
  • It has no accounts, log-in, chat or ways to talk to other players.
  • It does not use analytics or tracking, and it does not build advertising profiles. The optional ads described below are never personalised.
  • We do not sell any data. The only information that leaves your device is the technical information Google's ad software receives when an ad is loaded or shown (see Advertising).

Notifications

If you switch on Reminders in the pause menu, the game schedules local notifications on your device (for example, when a gift is ready). They are created on the device itself; no data leaves it. You can switch them off at any time in the game or in your device settings.

Sharing

The Share button opens your device's own share sheet with a line of text about your run. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Advertising

The game can show optional "rewarded" ads: you only see one if you tap a button marked WATCH AD (for example to revive once more, or to double a Lantern's Gift). The game never shows an ad on its own, and you never have to watch one.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. Because the game is designed for all ages, including children, every ad request is marked as child-directed: ads are not personalised, there is no remarketing, ad content is limited to a G (general audiences) rating, and the game does not use or send your device's Advertising ID.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad software automatically receives some technical information from your device: its IP address (which can show your approximate location), basic device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. This information goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's policies for child-directed ads: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and AdMob and Google Play's Families policy. We do not receive it, and we do not combine it with anything else.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and follows Google Play's Families policy. The game itself collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). The optional ads are child-directed and non-personalised for every player, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
